One core claim

Commit once. Keep the cache. Preserve quality.

The cache trap

Step-by-step routing can switch models after the history has grown. The new model cannot reuse the old model’s cached prefix, so the shared context is billed at the full input rate again.

Our policy

An L2-regularized classifier reads only Call 1: has_reasoning, has_input_image, and task_category. It commits the full trajectory to the cheap sibling or logged model.
